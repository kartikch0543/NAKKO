How NAKKO works

From booking to completion — designed to be fast, fair, and friction-free for everyone.

1
Users

Post your requirement

Describe the service you need, set your location, preferred time, and budget. Takes less than 2 minutes.

2
Workers

Workers accept & arrive

Nearby verified workers see your request and accept. You get real-time updates and worker profile before they arrive.

3
Both

Complete & pay securely

Work gets done, you confirm completion, payment processes automatically. Leave a review to help the community.

Platform features

Built for trust and transparency

Verified Workers

Every worker on NAKKO goes through identity verification and skill assessment before being listed on the platform.

Real-time Tracking

Track your worker's location in real time from acceptance to arrival. No more waiting without knowing.

Fair & Secure Payments

Pay only when the job is done. Funds are held in escrow and released on completion — protecting both sides.

Cooperative Model

NAKKO operates as a cooperative — workers earn fairly, have a voice, and share in the platform's success.

Earnings Dashboard

Workers get a full earnings breakdown, job history, and performance analytics to track their growth over time.

In-app Messaging

Communicate directly with your worker or customer through encrypted in-app chat — no need to share phone numbers.
